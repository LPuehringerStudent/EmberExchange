The Idea

Digital stove trading, made into a game.

EmberExchange combines collecting, economy, social features, and casino-style mini-games into one web app.

  • 1Players start with Coal, open lootboxes, and receive random stove collectibles.
  • 2Every stove has rarity, heat, collection value, and market history.
  • 3The goal is simple: build the best collection and grow your fortune.
Core Loop

Four steps that keep the game moving.

Open

Spend Coal on lootboxes and reveal stoves from Common to Secret rarity.

Trade

Sell duplicates, buy missing pieces, and negotiate with friends.

Upgrade

Use the Forge and Sparks to improve your collection and heat levels.

Play

Use your Coal in Poker, Blackjack, and Roulette for more risk and reward.

Lootboxes

Every box is a small moment of suspense.

Players open animated chests and receive stoves with different rarities. Higher rarity means higher value and more bragging rights.

  • CCommon stoves are easy to get and useful for forging.
  • LLegendary and Secret stoves are rare, valuable, and visually special.
  • HHeat levels add another layer of value beyond rarity.

Marketplace

Prices are set by players, not by us.

The marketplace lets players list stoves, buy instantly, inspect item history, and see what the community thinks something is worth.

List

Players choose the price for their own stove listings.

Buy

Other players can buy listings directly with Coal.

History

Price graphs help players understand value over time.

Trust

Sold states, ownership transfer, and item details make trades clear.

Investing

A market view for stove collectors.

The investing page turns item prices into something players can follow like a small stock market.

  • GGraphs show price movement and trends.
  • VPlayers can compare rarity, demand, and history.
  • SSmart trading becomes part of the gameplay.

Socials

Trading is better when players can talk.

The social system brings friends, chat, marketplace conversations, read states, and direct trade offers together.

Friends

Add friends, see conversations, and keep marketplace chat separate.

Trade Offers

Send offers directly inside chat, inspect stoves, accept, or decline.

Read State

Unread counts, last message previews, and read signs make chats feel complete.

Forge And Collections

Duplicates still matter.

Players can turn extra stoves into progress instead of letting them sit unused.

  • 6xSix stoves of one rarity can become one stove of the next rarity.
  • SUnwanted stoves can be salvaged into Sparks.
  • +Collections reward players for completing themed stove sets.
Mini-Games

Real-time games give Coal more purpose.

Poker, Blackjack, and Roulette let players risk Coal, compete with others, and keep the economy active.

Poker

Blackjack

Hit, stand, double, and try to get closest to 21.

Roulette

Pick a number, color, or range and watch the wheel decide.

Technology

Built as a full-stack web app.

The project is not just UI. It includes database logic, authentication, real-time communication, security, and deployment.

Angular Frontend

Standalone components, routing, services, signals, and a polished responsive interface.

Express Backend

TypeScript REST API with service layers for players, stoves, marketplace, chat, and games.

PostgreSQL

Persistent storage for accounts, inventory, trades, prices, messages, and game data.

WebSockets

Live chat, game rooms, read receipts, and real-time multiplayer state.

Security

Sessions, rate limiting, Turnstile, IP bans, honeypots, request logs, and admin protections.

Deployment

Production-ready build served through Render with health checks and scheduled jobs.

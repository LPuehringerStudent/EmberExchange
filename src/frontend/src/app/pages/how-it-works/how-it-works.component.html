Economy

Two Currencies, Endless Strategy

Every coin and spark has a purpose. Spend wisely, trade smart, and grow your empire.

Coins (Coal)

The main currency of EmberExchange. Use coins to open lootboxes, buy stoves on the marketplace, place bets in mini-games, and complete trades.

  • Open lootboxes
  • Buy & sell on the market
  • Bet in Poker, Blackjack & Roulette

Sparks

Earned by salvaging unwanted stoves. Sparks let you re-roll a stove's heat level and appear as prizes on the daily spin wheel.

  • Re-roll heat levels
  • Daily spin rewards

The Forge

Turn Six Into Something Legendary

The Forge lets you gamble for power. Take six stoves of the same rarity, melt them down, and create one stove of the next rarity tier. The risk is real — but so is the reward.

6× Same rarity
1× Higher rarity

Got duplicates? Salvage them for Sparks instead and use those sparks to re-roll a stove's heat level.

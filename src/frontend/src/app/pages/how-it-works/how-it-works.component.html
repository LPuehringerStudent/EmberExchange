How It Works

Four simple steps from your first lootbox to the high-stakes mini-games.

1

Open Lootboxes

Buy lootboxes with coal and crack them open to reveal rare and legendary stoves. Every box is a chance to score something epic.

2

Collect & Trade

Build your personal collection and trade duplicates with other players on the marketplace. Prices are set by the community, not us.

3

Upgrade (Forge)

Take six stoves of the same rarity and melt them down in the Forge to create a single, more powerful stove. Risky, but the rewards are worth it.

4

Play Mini-Games

Use your coal to play Poker, Blackjack, and Roulette against friends or the house. Win big and reinvest in your collection.
